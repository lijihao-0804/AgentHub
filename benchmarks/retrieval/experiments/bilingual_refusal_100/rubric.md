# Bilingual first-response QA adjudication

## What each question tests

Each of the 100 questions has one knowledge snapshot containing one public encyclopedia passage in the question’s language. Every question uses a different underlying article. English and Chinese each contribute 25 questions with sufficient evidence and 25 that require abstention. The authors wrote the questions for these passages before either arm saw them. The Chinese questions and sources were selected independently of the English ones. Examples include distinguishing an old stadium’s capacity from its replacement’s, assigning a composition credit and a lyric credit to different people, and subtracting two airport distances. A negative assertion that the passage explicitly supports is an answerable fact.

The English documents use one complete English Wikipedia paragraph from each of 50 different MuSiQue answerable training records. The selected paragraph is a distractor in its original record. The Chinese documents use complete Chinese Wikipedia passages from 50 different CMRC 2018 training articles. Titles and passage text reproduce the cached editions, including source punctuation and occasional extraction artifacts. Dates, entities and claims are judged against those editions. Only the title, a blank line, the passage and a final newline enter the knowledge snapshot. Each materialized entry links to its corresponding language edition of Wikipedia. The release files and locators identify the frozen passages if the Wikipedia pages subsequently change. [MuSiQue release and attribution](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique/tree/922ac98f19a201998dbdae6d7f2887a5258dbdeb) · [CMRC 2018 release, attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://github.com/ymcui/cmrc2018/tree/c0eb1b6ba219847457e6af3180da722bbeb656af) · [CMRC 2018 dataset paper](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1600/)

The historical audit checks question strings, source identifiers, article titles and passage fingerprints, including the prior 100-question study. It expands all MuSiQue development and test records, plus historical training records, to every paragraph. It compares the Chinese training selections against CMRC development and trial passages. English topic aliases recorded for the Chinese articles support cross-language title checks. The author also compared the two sets of topics and inspected the upstream training questions on the same articles for paraphrases. Exact question checks cover both training corpora and the historical question fields. Held-out split checks use source titles and passage hashes. Private uses, model pretraining exposure and every possible alternative title cannot be independently enumerated.

The frozen case rows contain the question, language, target, disposition, source locator, source digest and acceptable answer facets. For each facet, half-open character offsets count Unicode code points in the decoded document, including title and newlines. A span preserves the relation needed to evaluate the facet. Some targets require two source quantities and simple arithmetic. An explicitly listed neutral short form may consist of a number, code or ordered pair of codes. All gold fields stay outside model inputs.

## Assessment of each observed response

Assess the first physical provider response and the final endpoint response separately. Use the parsed state, visible answer and actual cited excerpts. An error, truncated or structurally invalid response, dangling marker or guess in the answer state on an unanswerable case earns zero strict credit. Missing observations retain their own count. A correction cannot replace a first attempt.

1. **Answer content.** Check every requested fact, role, unit, scope and qualification. Accept an equivalent expression or calculation supported by the passage. On a paired question, check which value belongs to each entity. An extra material claim requires support. A contradiction, invented detail or fact attached to the wrong entity fails content. On an unanswerable case, the refusal state must communicate that the requested information cannot be established from the supplied evidence, without a guess or a definitive negative inferred from silence.
2. **Citations.** Every factual claim in an answer must be linked to an actual numbered excerpt that supports it in context. Excerpts may jointly support a multi-part answer or a calculation. A matching number from another role, an irrelevant additional citation or a true claim supported only by text outside the cited excerpts fails citation correctness. Every displayed marker must identify a returned citation. Correct abstention uses the refusal state, an empty citation list and no marker. An abstention in the answer state fails the contract.
3. **Response language.** The ordinary explanation or refusal must use the language of the question. Names, conventional codes, units and cited phrases may retain their source form within an otherwise appropriate response. A complete, unambiguously language-neutral short answer, such as `1893 [1]` to a question asking for a year, counts as compatible. Evaluate language on the original visible text, including readable text in a structurally invalid response. A response such as “证据不足。” to an English question can correctly recognize a gap and still fail language. English prose attached to an isolated quoted Chinese term remains English. Inspect bilingual or ambiguous prose manually. When no answer text is available, the reporting layer records language as `null` and retains the observation in the strict denominator with zero credit.
4. **Strict credit.** Require correct content, correct citations and compatible language together. Report the component judgments separately, including an otherwise correct refusal delivered in the other language. For answerable questions track both absence of required evidence in the context and refusal despite sufficient visible evidence. The full scoped passage determines the gold disposition. Evidence selection explains a failure without changing that label.

## Blinded review

Shuffle and de-identify each observation. Show the reviewer the original question, language, disposition, target, complete document, visible evidence, actual response state, full answer and its cited excerpts. Keep arm names, prompt text, ordering by arm and running totals concealed. Identical responses with the same question, state, evidence and citations may share a judgement. Preserve the mapping from a blinded review identifier to individual stage and arm occurrences separately.

The automatic evaluator screens aliases, actual cited support, refusal wording and language. It flags all free answers and all noncanonical refusals for semantic inspection. Give equivalent wording credit after reading the actual excerpt. Check role swaps, negation, arithmetic, unsupported additional claims and reply language. Record content, citation and language booleans independently and give a reason for overrides. Resolve genuine ambiguity with an independent reviewer before unblinding, or record the unresolved cases and their denominator. Preserve the automatic scores. Release only the agreed derived assessments and keep raw provider responses, source corpus files and receipts in the local experiment area.

## Reproduction

From the repository root, run `python -m benchmarks.retrieval.experiments.bilingual_refusal_100.dataset --fetch-cmrc` to obtain any missing pinned CMRC release files and create the local upload manifest. The MuSiQue cache layout and official release URL are in `benchmarks/public_rag/sources.json`. With both caches present, the dataset command runs offline without `--fetch-cmrc`. Run `python -m benchmarks.retrieval.experiments.bilingual_refusal_100.verify` for document, gold and evaluator checks. Add `--history` to compare the complete local exposure audit with the frozen receipt tree. A checkout without the locally retained historical receipts can still reconstruct all 100 documents and run the gold checks.
