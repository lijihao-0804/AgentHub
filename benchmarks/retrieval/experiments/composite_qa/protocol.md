Break out deterministic eligibility and actual changes as well as all 24 pairs. A change in packing does not itself constitute improved candidate recall.

For every observation report the first physical response separately from final HTTP delivery. Inspect the answer, cited passages, provenance, question language and disposition. Count missing or structurally invalid attempts as strict failures. Citation support requires that all material claims are supported by what the reader can actually retrieve from the numbered citation. Verify component source offsets, both chunk IDs and any supporting text beyond character 2,000. Preserve automatic grades and blind-review free responses and ambiguous cases.

Replay the 100 historical R questions, 100 B questions and 14 W3C table questions through the candidate. Protect every previously successful question and each successful stage in the archived arms, including R092 and B006. An archived comparison can differ in prompt as well as sampled model behavior; identify that limitation for each regression. An unchanged wire input on a historical control establishes equivalence of the evidence sent, not equivalence of newly generated answers.

Advance the retrieval default only with measurable gain in complete evidence delivered or correctly supported first responses on the independent primary pairs, zero previously successful first/final regressions, no loss of already delivered complete evidence, correct refusal/citation/locale preservation, and no unverified source identity. Stress-only gains, a fuller candidate pool or the smaller dense-encoder experiment do not qualify. Keep independent engineering fixes separately mergeable.
