# First-response QA scoring rules

## Population

Each of the 100 new English questions has its own public source and its own single-document knowledge snapshot. Both arms of a question use that snapshot. A document consists of the title followed by one complete cached Wikipedia paragraph from MuSiQue or one complete cached paper abstract from QASPER. Fifty questions have a supported answer. Fifty ask for an unstated condition, relation, measurement, name or other fact. A negative answer explicitly supported by the source counts as an answerable question. An absence label applies only to this selected passage or abstract.

The 60 MuSiQue documents come from the answerable TRAIN v1.0 file. The 40 QASPER documents come from TRAIN v0.3. Each contributes equal numbers of answerable and unanswerable questions. The experiment authors wrote all questions after reading the texts, before seeing arm outputs. Categories and source proportions are fixed by design. Both corpora are English and public, and the model may already know facts about the subjects. No population prevalence or wider retrieval result follows from this sample.

The committed case manifest records the source locator, document digest, question, expected disposition and gold answer or reason for abstention. An answerable case also defines one or more answer components with allowed short forms and supporting character offsets in the UTF-8-decoded document. Offsets use half-open Unicode character intervals. The source material stays in the ignored local cache. Source links and credit are available in the materialized manifest. Dataset attribution and licensing are recorded by the original distributors. [MuSiQue, Trivedi et al.](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique) · [QASPER, Dasigi et al.](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/qasper)

## First-attempt and endpoint units

Evaluate the first physical provider response for each of the 200 arm–question combinations. Preserve a separate assessment of the response returned by the HTTP endpoint. Use the actual excerpt associated with each numbered citation and the actual response state. Error and structurally invalid output earn zero joint credit. A missing response is a missing measurement. Record its count instead of counting it as a valid refusal. Replay, correction and second attempts cannot replace a first response.

## Blinded semantic assessment

Give the reviewer the question, its full scoped document, expected disposition, target, actual response state, complete answer, and the actual numbered excerpts cited by that answer. If available, also provide the other evidence visible to the model for diagnosing retrieval. Conceal arm identity, provider/system prompts, file ordering by arm and running score. Review each distinct response once per identical question, state, answer, cited excerpts and visible evidence. Carry each judgement back to every occurrence using a private key recorded before review. Preserve the unedited automatic scores.

1. **Answerable question.** Require an answer in the product's answer state. Check every requested fact, role, unit, direction and condition. Accept an accurate equivalent expression, a directly entailed inference or an unambiguous equivalent unit. For a comparison or multiple values, the assignment to each entity matters. A correct number attached to the wrong entity fails. A textual refusal fails on a supported negative claim. A material contradiction or a fabricated additional factual assertion prevents joint credit.
2. **Citation support.** Check what clause each actual marker refers to. Inspect its own excerpt for support of that clause, including the entity, relation, qualifier and units. Multiple citations may jointly cover distinct required facts. Every factual claim needs adequate support and every displayed citation must support a claim it accompanies. Quoting a number from an unrelated role, citing the right document with an irrelevant excerpt, or adding an irrelevant citation fails citation correctness. Extraneous true claims also need support in the cited excerpts. Source text outside those excerpts cannot rescue the citation. Equivalent supporting wording and alternative valid evidence are accepted on blinded review.
3. **Unanswerable question.** Award joint credit only for a valid refusal with an empty citation list and no inline marker. The answer may explain what the passage omits. A guessed value, a named person unsupported in that role, a definitive negative inferred from silence, or a claim that an adjacent fact answers the question fails. Record a semantic abstention carried in the answer state separately when it occurs. The endpoint contract requires the refusal state.
4. **Stage diagnosis.** If the complete document supports an answer but the visible evidence omits required support, retain the answerable gold disposition. Mark the missing evidence as a retrieval or context-selection limitation. Track refusal on visible support separately from this case. A source with an explicitly stated negative does provide support. The question's phrasing alone provides no proof.
5. **Review output.** Record separate answer correctness, citation correctness and their conjunction. Record whether each factual claim has a supporting citation and provide a short reason for a failure or an automated-score override. Genuine uncertainty goes to an independent adjudicator before arm labels are revealed. Preserve unresolved rows and their denominators if adjudication cannot resolve them.

## Automated checks

`score_case` checks state, marker agreement, answer-component aliases and the presence of source-bound support spans in each excerpt attached to a matching claim fragment. It flags every valid free-form answer and noncanonical refusal for semantic review. The automatic score is a reproducible preliminary score. It cannot settle negation, the assignment of two quantities, explanatory claims added by a model, or every valid paraphrase. The published semantic result uses the preceding blind rules.

`evidence_covers` checks the visible evidence for frozen support spans independently of the decision to answer. An abbreviated excerpt can convey the same fact with a shorter span. A reviewer may mark it sufficient if the excerpt itself establishes the relation. The current population is 100 short, single-source tasks. It measures first answer and refusal behavior given these scopes.

From the repository root, run `python -m benchmarks.retrieval.experiments.first_refusal_100.dataset` to materialize the documents and local manifest, and `python -m benchmarks.retrieval.experiments.first_refusal_100.verify --history` to verify annotations, source identity, evaluator regressions and recorded historical overlap. The exposure audit covers the historical public-RAG result JSON/JSONL files available at freeze time, including locally retained receipts and published projections. It checks QASPER paper and official question identifiers, and MuSiQue parent records, paragraph titles and content fingerprints. It also tests for equality with normalized upstream training questions. Private, deleted or untraceable external uses lie outside this audit.
